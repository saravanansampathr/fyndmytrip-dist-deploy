Flights, hotels, buses and holidays, booked in minutes

FyndMyTrip brings every part of a trip into one place. Search live fares across airlines, compare hotels and bus operators, and pay once with UPI, cards, netbanking or your wallet.

Every price you see includes taxes. Your e-ticket or voucher reaches your email and mobile as soon as you pay.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book without creating an account?

Yes. Flights, hotels, buses and holiday packages can be booked as a guest with just an email and mobile number. Sign in if you want to use your wallet balance or see all your trips in one place.

How do I pay?

You can pay by UPI, credit or debit card, netbanking, international cards or your wallet balance. Payments go through a secure, PCI-DSS compliant gateway.

Where do I find my ticket?

Your e-ticket or voucher is emailed to you and can be downloaded from the confirmation page. Signed-in customers also find it under My Trips.

I am a travel agent. Can I book for my clients?

Yes. Apply for a B2B account on our Partner page to get agent fares, a credit wallet, your own markups and a white-label booking site.
