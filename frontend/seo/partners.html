Frequently asked questions

Who can apply for a B2B account?

Registered travel agencies, tour operators, corporates with a travel desk and online travel resellers can apply. Keep your PAN, GSTIN (if registered) and business proof ready.

How do I pay for bookings?

Agents book from a prepaid or credit wallet, topped up by bank transfer, UPI or card. Every top-up and booking shows in your ledger.

Can my clients see my markup?

No. Your clients see one fare with your markup included. You can also print tickets without a fare for your clients.

Do you offer a white-label website?

Yes. Choose from more than 50 website designs, use your own domain and logo, and your customers book directly with you.

Is there an API?

Yes. Our partner API covers search, booking, ticketing and cancellation, with sandbox keys for testing.
