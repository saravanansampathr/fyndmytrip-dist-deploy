Frequently asked questions

Do the fares include taxes?

Yes. The price on each flight is the total per traveller including airline taxes and fees. The fare summary at checkout shows the full breakup before you pay.

Can I choose my seat and meal?

Yes. After entering traveller details you can pick seats from the seat map and add meals, extra baggage and other services offered by the airline.

Are special fares available?

Student, senior citizen and armed forces fares are offered where the airline supports them. Carry the matching ID proof when you travel.

When does web check-in open?

Most airlines open web check-in 48 hours before departure for domestic flights and 24 hours for international flights. The confirmation page links to the airline check-in page.
