Frequently asked questions

Is the price per night or for the whole stay?

Results show the price for your dates. The checkout summary shows the total for every room and night, with taxes included.

What do I show at check-in?

Show your hotel voucher, on your phone or printed, along with a government photo ID for each adult guest.

Can I cancel my hotel booking?

Each room shows its cancellation rules before you book. Free-cancellation rooms can be cancelled from My Trips until the deadline shown.
