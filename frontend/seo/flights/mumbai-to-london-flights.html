Frequently asked questions

Which airport do Mumbai to London flights use?

Flights leave from Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) and arrive at London Heathrow Airport (LHR).

How do I find the cheapest Mumbai to London flight?

Search your date, sort by price and look at nearby dates. Saver fares are usually the cheapest; flexible fares cost more but cost less to change.

Can I cancel or change my ticket?

Yes, as the airline's fare rules allow. Each fare shows whether it is refundable before you book, and you can cancel from My Trips.

When does web check-in open?

Most airlines open web check-in 24 hours before departure. The confirmation page links to the airline check-in page.
